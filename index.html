FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Contract Breakpoint Analysis assess?
It assesses whether an execution-sensitive commercial agreement can preserve contractual protection under predictable operational pressure before signature.
How is it different from legal contract review?
Legal review addresses legal validity and enforceability. Contract Breakpoint Analysis addresses whether contractual protection remains usable when operational, timing and procedural dependencies move under stress.
When should it be used?
Only before signature, while the agreement remains under draft, legal review, commercial alignment or final approval.
What does the buyer receive?
A written Contract Breakpoint Analysis Report for one specific agreement, containing agreement-specific findings and their contractual consequences under predictable execution pressure.
What agreements qualify?
Execution-sensitive commercial agreements with material downside and dependencies in delivery, timing, quality, documentation or escalation.
Can one analysis be reused?
No. One agreement equals one analysis. The output cannot be reused across contracts or counterparties.
How much does it cost?
€12,000 fixed fee per agreement, subject to eligibility. No hourly billing, discounts or bundles.
How is it delivered?
Written-only. No calls, workshops or methodology transfer.
What if the agreement has already been signed?
Contract Breakpoint Analysis no longer applies. Contract Execution Protection is a separate post-signature engagement for one signed contract during an active execution-risk window.
