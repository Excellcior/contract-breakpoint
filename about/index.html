I built Contract Breakpoint for one specific pre-signature problem: identifying where an execution-sensitive commercial agreement may fail to preserve contractual protection when operational conditions move.

I also publish Execution Notes: editorial analysis of public operational events where execution conditions and commercial or contractual structure stop moving together.
