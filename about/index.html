My background spans more than 25 years across procurement, logistics, shipping and commercial operations.

Contract Breakpoint Analysis is used before signature on one specific commercial contract. It identifies where predictable operational incidents can break the agreement, trigger escalation failures or undermine claims and recoverability. It does not predict whether those incidents will occur.

CBA is not legal advice, contract drafting or renegotiation, an operational audit, a pricing review, a counterparty credit assessment or a reusable risk report.

One contract is analysed once, before signature.

I also publish Execution Notes: editorial analyses of public operational events and their contractual consequences. Execution Notes are not Contract Breakpoint Analysis outputs and are not legal advice.
