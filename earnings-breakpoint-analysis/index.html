THE GAP

Maritime credit covers the iron and the paper. Rarely the charter.

Maritime credit teams typically cover two of the relevant layers: the asset through technical survey and the borrower through credit analysis.

The remaining layer is the execution structure of the charter generating the hire behind debt service.

EBA examines that layer structurally under defined execution conditions.

SCOPE

What Earnings Breakpoint Analysis reads.

  • Charter party and off-hire / laytime exposure
  • Payment-latency exposure in the charter structure
  • Documentary chain behind the hire servicing the loan
  • Force majeure provisions capable of interrupting the charterer → owner → lender cash-flow chain
  • Structural points where hire cash flow fails under defined execution conditions before the deterioration appears in the DSCR
OUTPUT

A written assessment for one facility or name.

The deliverable is an Earnings Breakpoint Analysis Report covering findings specific to the charter or facility, the defined execution conditions under which hire cash flow structurally fails, and the resulting consequence for debt service.

It is not credit analysis, a technical survey or a legal opinion.

It addresses the operational execution layer between them.

EBA does not predict whether the defined conditions will occur.
