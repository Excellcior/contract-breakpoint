THE EXPOSURE

The contract is signed. The remaining exposure is procedural.

Execution can continue while contractual protection is lost through missed notice, timing, escalation, recipient or format requirements.

Execution Breakpoint Protection applies during that post-signature window while the relevant rights remain procedurally preservable.

SCOPE

What Execution Breakpoint Protection protects.

Notice

Procedural exposure where contractual protection depends on a required notice being preserved.

Timing

Deadlines and time-dependent procedural requirements during active execution.

Escalation

Required procedural escalation where rights depend on sequence or timing.

Recipient and format

Procedural exposure created by recipient, channel or format requirements.
