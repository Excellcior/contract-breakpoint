# CBA / EBP Learning Firewall

Version: 2026-09-10

CBA and EBP remain temporally separate. CBA outputs freeze at signature. There is no retroactive validation, contract-level evidentiary linkage, or use of an EBP outcome to prove CBA accuracy. CBA rigor may not justify EBP. Learning may move only through one-way abstraction with evidence containment; no methodology is disclosed.

## Public routing

A narrow public routing exception exists solely to move a visitor to the temporally applicable product:

- CBA may link to EBP only when the contract is already signed.
- EBP may discreetly link to CBA when the contract is not yet signed.

The exception is navigational only. EBA is not part of it.

## Cross-product justification

Public routing must never imply that one product proves the other, expose learning loops, connect a specific CBA finding to a later EBP event, market one product using outcomes from the other, or suggest shared evidentiary validation.
