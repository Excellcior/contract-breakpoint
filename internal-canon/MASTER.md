# Contract Breakpoint product canon

Version: 2026-09-10

## Contract Breakpoint Analysis (CBA)

Pre-signature analysis for one execution-sensitive commercial contract. The canonical fee remains €12,000 fixed fee per contract.

## Execution Breakpoint Protection (EBP)

Execution Breakpoint Protection is the canonical name of the Phase 2 product formerly called Contract Execution Protection (CEP). The rename does not expand or change its scope.

EBP is active after signature for one signed contract during an active execution-risk window while relevant contractual rights remain procedurally preservable. It provides written procedural protection concerning notice, timing, escalation, recipient and format requirements.

EBP is written-only. It provides no advice, contract interpretation, drafting, negotiation, operational management or counterparty communication.

Commercial structure: €2,500 monthly retainer per signed contract; no minimum term; cancelable; no tiers; no bundle; no setup fee.

## Earnings Breakpoint Analysis (EBA)

Name: Earnings Breakpoint Analysis.

Category: lender-side structural execution analysis.

Buyer: maritime private credit, funds and lessors, primarily middle-market.

Use window: pre-deal or watchlist.

Core determination: EBA identifies where the hire cash flow behind a maritime loan structurally fails under defined execution conditions.

Prediction boundary: EBA does not predict whether those conditions will occur, forecast charterer behavior or forecast payment probability.

Authority: EBA does not approve, reject or make a credit decision. Credit and investment authority remains with the lender.

Scope may include charter party exposure; off-hire / laytime exposure; payment-latency exposure in the charter structure; the documentary chain supporting hire; force majeure provisions affecting the charterer → owner → lender cash-flow chain; and structural hire cash-flow failure points under defined execution conditions.

Output: an Earnings Breakpoint Analysis Report containing findings specific to the relevant charter, name or facility, the defined execution conditions under which hire cash flow structurally fails, and the consequence for debt service.

EBA is not credit analysis, a rating, DSCR modelling, a credit decision, a technical survey, asset-condition assessment, legal advice, an enforceability opinion, a performance forecast or a counterparty-behaviour forecast.

Delivery: written-only.

Commercial structure: single-name read from USD 4,000; facility / portfolio read from USD 12,000. Scope and fee are agreed in writing before work begins.

Public exposure may state the product name, lender-side category, buyer/use window, structural scope at the approved level of abstraction, output, boundaries, fees and non-prediction rule. It must not expose methodology, taxonomy, scoring, internal reasoning, thresholds or reusable frameworks.

Public cross-reference: EBA remains isolated. There is no public product cross-reference to or from CBA or EBP, and the products are not publicly described as a family.

The only CBA ↔ EBP public cross-reference exception is temporal routing: CBA may route to EBP only after signature, and EBP may route to CBA only before signature. The exception is navigational only.
